Consist: Train Cars

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 27, 2026

Consist is an Apple Watch app that shows MBTA trains and buses near you and helps you keep track of the cars you've ridden. It does not have accounts, ads, or analytics, and the developer does not collect any of your information.

Location

If you allow it, Consist uses your watch's location to open the nearest station, find nearby bus stops, and tap you before your stop while you ride. Your location is used on the watch. To find nearby stations and stops, the app sends your approximate coordinates to the MBTA's public data service (api-v3.mbta.com), the same way any MBTA trip planner does. Consist does not store your location history or send it anywhere else.

Your ride log and settings

The cars you log, your stops, and your settings are saved only on your watch. They are not uploaded to the developer.

MBTA API key

If you enter your own MBTA API key, it is stored on your watch and sent only to the MBTA with each data request.

Notifications

Ride alerts ("Next stop") are local notifications created on your watch. No push notification service is used.

Third parties

Train, bus, and crowding information comes from the MBTA's public V3 API. Consist is not affiliated with or endorsed by the MBTA. The MBTA's own privacy policy applies to requests sent to its service.

Children

Consist does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
