Consist: Train Cars

Support

Consist shows MBTA trains and buses on your Apple Watch: which cars are coming, which one to board, and a tap before your stop. Log every car you ride and chase the whole fleet.

Getting started

  1. Open Consist and allow location. It opens the station you're at.
  2. Tap a train to see its cars. Tap a car to log it.
  3. Tap Ride this. Pick your stop (or let Consist pick one of your stops) and you'll get a tap when it's next.

"MBTA is busy" / too many requests

Consist works without a key, but the MBTA limits how often keyless apps can ask for updates. A free key fixes this:

  1. On a phone or computer, go to api-v3.mbta.com.
  2. Tap Register and make a free account.
  3. Open your account and tap Request a new key.
  4. On your watch: Consist → Settings → MBTA data → API key. Type in the 32-character key.

Other settings

  • Your stops: the stations Consist highlights and picks for ride alerts, per line and direction.
  • Car suggestions: the white bar under a car. Choose newer cars, retro (older) cars, or your own mix of age, fleet, and where on the train. Less crowded cars win when the MBTA reports crowding.
  • Logo: pick a Red, Green, Blue, or Orange Line logo for the menu.
